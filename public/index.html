How we work

How Studios operates

Internal product practice, not a consultancy. Strategy through production for software that stays on our books.

  • 01
    Own the product We conceive, build, ship, and operate software we own. Year Wall, Wandered, SurroStack, and the rest sit under Burkett Studios.
  • 02
    Design under Burkett Identity Same design law as burkettinv.com: sharp geometry, gold ignition, production restraint. No second brand system for digital.
  • 03
    iOS and web production Native clients and web platforms run as real products with quality gates, not demos that never leave the lab.
  • 04
    Infrastructure we operate Cloudflare, deploy automation, and agent-operable paths so our products stay live and maintainable.
  • 05
    Company systems when needed When Burkett Investments needs a real product surface for ops, Studios builds and runs it under the same stack.

About

Part of Burkett Investments

Burkett Investments is multi-avenue. Consulting and investment management live on burkettinv.com. Burkett Studios is the digital product arm: we own the code, the domains, and the ongoing operation of the software listed here.

  • Owned digital assets Studios is not a services desk. We build, run, and own the portfolio: consumer apps, marketplaces, and platforms with Burkett on the title.
  • Same brand law as burkettinv.com Cybertruck geometry, gold ignition, hairline systems. Digital products inherit Burkett Identity, not a spin-off look.
  • Ship production, keep operating Live domains, git history, and ongoing ops. Launch is the start of ownership, not the end of a client engagement.
