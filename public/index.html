How we work

How Studios runs

An internal product practice. Strategy through production for software that stays on our books.

  • 01
    Own the product We invent, ship, and keep operating software under Burkett title. Launch is not a handoff to someone else.
  • 02
    One Identity system Studios products sit under Burkett design law: black, steel, gold ignition, sharp geometry. Same family as burkettinv.com.
  • 03
    Production surfaces iOS, web, and APIs with real deploy paths. Domains stay live; quality gates decide when mobile distribution opens.
  • 04
    Operate what we ship Cloudflare, auth, mail, and agent-operable deploy. Ownership includes the boring work after day one.
